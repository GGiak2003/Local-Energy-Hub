Who we are

Who We Are

Local Energy Hub is a community-focused initiative dedicated to improving renewable energy awareness, accessibility, and forecasting. Our goal is to empower households, businesses, and local organizations with clear, actionable energy insights.

Our mission

“We believe that sustainable energy should be understandable and accessible to everyone.”

Through real-time dashboards, forecasting tools, and educational resources, we help communities make informed decisions that reduce environmental impact and improve energy efficiency.
